Help Manual

  1. Tap a state to mark it. In cycle mode, repeated taps rotate through your active levels and back to unvisited.
  2. Switch to select mode in Settings if you prefer choosing a level and adding a quick dated note in one step.
  3. Open a state drawer by selecting a state. Use it to change the level and manage dated visit notes.
  4. Use the Legend to edit level names, definitions, and colors. Add optional built-in levels or your own custom levels.
  5. Use Import/Export to back up data or move it to another device.

FAQ

Where is my data saved?
In your browser localStorage on this device.

Can someone else use the same app link?
Yes. Their browser keeps a separate local copy of their data.

Does it work offline?
Yes. After the file is saved or loaded, the app does not need a backend or external service.

Can I move data to another device?
Export a JSON backup, then import it in the app on the other device.
